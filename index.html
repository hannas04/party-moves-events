The PartyMoves Studio

About Us

Every celebration deserves an entrance worthy of the moment.

PartyMoves Events began with a simple conviction: decor should feel choreographed, not scattered — every arch, drape, and centrepiece moving the eye toward one seamless story. Under the direction of founder Deji Awolola, our studio has since styled weddings, courtly traditional ceremonies, corporate activations, and milestone birthdays across Abuja and beyond.

We don't work from a catalogue. Each brief is treated as a new choreography — built around your palette, your venue, and the feeling you want your guests to carry home with them.

"A party isn't just planned. It's moved." Deji Awolola — Creative Director & Founder

How We Work

The Four Moves

Every PartyMoves event follows the same four movements — a rhythm refined over 150+ events, so nothing is left to chance on your day.

Move One

Discover

A consultation to learn your story, your venue, your guests and your budget.

Move Two

Design

Mood boards, palette and a full concept, refined with you until every detail feels right.

Move Three

Style

Our team builds, dresses and styles the venue — while you focus on the day itself.

Move Four

Reveal

The room opens, your guests step in, and the celebration takes over.
